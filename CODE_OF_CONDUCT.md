# Community code of conduct

Be respectful and focus on the work. Welcome questions from people with different backgrounds and experience. Give specific, constructive feedback and respect other people's privacy.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing and publishing private credentials or personal information are not acceptable. Do not use issues or discussions to advertise unrelated products or repeatedly pressure maintainers for unpaid support.

The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive threads, or restrict participation. To request moderation, open an issue asking for a private contact without posting personal details. Security vulnerabilities belong in [private vulnerability reporting](SECURITY.md). This community policy does not promise confidentiality for information posted publicly.
