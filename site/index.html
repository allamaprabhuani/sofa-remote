What ships today

Your Mac. A little closer.

  • Touchpad

    Click, right-click, double-click, drag and scroll with two fingers. Move the pointer across all your displays.

  • Keyboard

    Type into the active Mac window, or pause your music and adjust the volume.

  • Shortcuts

    Saved launchers for websites and apps, so one tap opens the thing you came for.

  • Display & windows

    Move the front window to another display. Turn the screens off and wake them while the Mac stays awake.

  • Four themes

    Imperial, Light, Ocean, or your own colour. Choose what feels right on your phone.

  • Free & open source

    MIT-licensed. No accounts, ads, analytics, subscriptions, or screen streaming.

Setup

Open. Scan. Connect.

  1. 01

    Open the DMG, drag Sofa Remote to Applications, grant Accessibility when macOS asks.

  2. 02

    Open the menu-bar icon and scan its QR code with your iPhone's camera. Safari opens the remote.

  3. 03

    Add to Home Screen from Safari's Share menu. Keep both devices on the same trusted Wi-Fi, or route them through your own Tailscale network.

Full written guide: docs/SETUP.md.

Before you install
  • Ad-hoc signed and not notarised; macOS may block the first launch. The setup guide shows how to allow it.
  • Accessibility permission is required so the helper can send clicks and keystrokes. Screen Recording is not needed.
  • Direct LAN is unencrypted HTTP. Use a trusted Wi-Fi or route devices through your own Tailscale network.
  • The Mac must stay awake. The remote can sleep or wake the display, but cannot wake a sleeping Mac or unlock it.
  • Expect the occasional pointer jitter and some provider-side quirks — see Known Issues.

Good to know

A few answers.

Does it mirror or record my screen?

No. Sofa Remote only sends input — pointer, keystrokes, media keys. There is no screen capture, no recording, no streaming, and no remote-view window.

Do I need an account or cloud service?

No. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no analytics, no telemetry. The phone talks to the Mac on your local network (or your own Tailscale network).

Is it secure?

Over direct LAN the transport is plain HTTP — fine on a trusted home Wi-Fi, not safe against a hostile network. For anything else, point both devices at your own Tailscale network for encrypted transport.

Can it wake or unlock my Mac?

No. It can sleep or wake the display, but the Mac itself has to be awake and signed in. There's no unlock bypass.

Any paid tier, upsell or ads?

No. It's MIT-licensed and free. There's a Ko-fi link if you want to buy a coffee, but nothing is gated.
