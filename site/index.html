What it does

Touchpad with click, right-click, double-click, drag and two-finger scroll. A keyboard that types Unicode into whatever Mac window has focus. Media and volume keys. Saved shortcuts for websites and apps. Cross-display pointer with an explicit window move. Sleep or wake the display while the Mac stays awake.

Four lightweight themes ship in beta 2: Imperial (default), Light, Ocean, and a custom colour picker.

Setup

  1. 01

    Open the DMG, drag Sofa Remote to Applications, and grant Accessibility when macOS asks.

  2. 02

    Open the menu-bar icon and scan its QR code with your iPhone's camera; Safari opens the remote.

  3. 03

    Add it to your Home Screen from Safari's Share menu, on the same trusted Wi-Fi or your Tailscale network.

Full written guide: docs/SETUP.md.

Before you install

  • Ad-hoc signed and not notarised; macOS may block the first launch — the setup guide shows how to allow it.
  • Accessibility permission is required so the helper can send clicks and keystrokes.
  • Direct LAN is unencrypted HTTP; use a trusted Wi-Fi or route devices through your own Tailscale network.
  • The Mac must stay awake. The remote can sleep or wake the display but cannot wake a sleeping Mac or unlock it.
  • Occasional pointer jitter and provider compatibility caveats apply — see Known Issues.
